Questions

Is Fusecore free?

Yes. The full 60-wave campaign on Easy and Normal, daily ops and friend challenges are free. A one-time Commander Edition unlocks Hard mode, Endless and more. An optional Command Pass adds daily drops and a season track.

Are there ads or energy timers?

No. There are no ads and no energy. You can play as long as you like.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Campaign runs work fully offline. Daily boards and friend challenges need a connection.

Can I pay to win?

No. Nothing that decides a competitive score is for sale. Daily Ops and challenges use the same standard loadout for everyone.
