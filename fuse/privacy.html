Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026. This policy covers "Fusecore: Merge Defense" and its browser edition.

The short version

No tracking. No advertising. No analytics SDKs. No account, name, email or phone number is collected. Your progress is stored on your device.

Data stored on your device

Game progress, settings, unlocked items and run history are saved locally on your device (app storage, or your browser local storage for the web edition). We cannot see this data. Deleting the app or clearing site data removes it.

Optional online features

If you open Daily Ops boards or friend challenges, the app contacts our backend, hosted on Supabase (a database service). It receives only:

  • an anonymous, randomly generated device token (not linked to your Apple ID, name or advertising identifier);
  • an auto-generated callsign (you cannot type free text, so no user-generated content is shown);
  • your run scores and challenge codes, with the game version and a timestamp.

The hosting provider may process technical data such as IP addresses to deliver the service and prevent abuse. This data is not used for advertising and is not sold or shared with third parties for marketing. Offline play sends nothing.

Purchases

Purchases and subscriptions are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. The app only receives confirmation that an item is owned, in order to unlock it.

Children

Fusecore is a strategy game with no ads and no free-text chat. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Deletion and your rights

To delete your online data (device token, callsign and scores), email alhassan.mu@gmail.com and include the callsign shown in Settings. We will delete it within 30 days. Depending on where you live you may also have rights of access, correction and objection; contact us to use them. Local data can be removed any time by deleting the app.

Changes and contact

If we change this policy, the date above will be updated. Contact: alhassan.mu@gmail.com.
