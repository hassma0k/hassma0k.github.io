Questions

Is it free to play?

Yes, with no time limit. Small and Medium maps, Easy, Normal and Hard AI, four blocs, Colonel Rashid, the first two Operation missions, the Weekly Challenge, two-player Pass & Play and joining Friend Matches are all free. There are no ads and no subscription. Purchases are optional.

What can I buy?

Everything else is an optional one-time purchase: Commander Edition (all six blocs, Large and Huge maps, Elite AI, 3 and 4 player wars, hosting Friend Matches), Founder’s Edition (everything, now and in the future), the blocs Cyber Vanguard and Orbital Command, General Falcon, General Thunder, General Broker, the War Council, Operation Strait Shield and the Recruit’s Kit. Nothing is consumed, and Family Sharing is on.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Solo matches, Operations and Pass & Play work with no connection. Signing in is optional and only needed for Friend Match and the Weekly Challenge leaderboard.

Is it pay-to-win?

No. In Weekly Challenge and Friend Match every General is on the same level, and AI bonuses are shown before the match starts.

I bought it on another device. How do I restore?

Tap Restore Purchases in the Armory or in Settings while signed into the same Apple ID.
