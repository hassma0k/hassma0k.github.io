Questions

Is it free to play?

Yes. The core game is free, with no ads and no timers. A short match fits in about 15 minutes.

What does the Commander Edition unlock?

A single one-time purchase unlocks all map sizes, harder AI, extra modes, online friend matches and premium blocs. There is no subscription and nothing is ever consumed.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Solo matches and Pass & Play work with no connection. Sign-in is optional and only needed for online features.

I bought it on another device. How do I restore?

Open Settings and tap Restore Purchases while signed into the same Apple ID.
