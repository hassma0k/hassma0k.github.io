How it works

Fair Value uses the income-capitalization method: net yearly income (revenue minus costs) divided by the target yield a buyer expects for the asset's risk and management effort. Riskier, harder-to-run assets need a higher return, which lowers the fair price.

Who it's for

  • Buyers taking over an existing shop or small business in Saudi Arabia.
  • Property investors checking whether an apartment price makes sense for its rent.
  • Franchise buyers comparing fees and royalties with expected income.
  • Brokers and advisors who need a quick, defensible number.
