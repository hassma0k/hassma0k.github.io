Frequently asked questions

Is the app free?

Yes. Fair value, range, explanation, examples and up to 3 saved deals are free. Pro is an optional one-time purchase of SAR 29.99, with no subscription.

How is the fair value calculated?

With the income-capitalization method: net yearly income (revenue minus costs) divided by the return a buyer expects for the risk and management effort. More risk or effort means a lower fair price.

Is this a certified valuation?

No. It is an indicative estimate based on your numbers, to help you negotiate. For financing or official purposes, use a valuer licensed by the Saudi Authority for Accredited Valuers (Taqeem).

Do I need an account or internet?

No. The app runs fully on your phone, offline and without an account, and collects no data.

Is there an Android version?

The app is iPhone-only for now. The quick calculator on this page works on any device.

How do I restore my purchase?

In the app, open the ☰ menu → Restore purchases, using the same Apple ID.
