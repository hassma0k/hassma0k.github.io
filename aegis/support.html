How to play

  1. Enemies enter at the glowing portal and follow the shortest path to your Core. Tap a build card, then a tile, to see a ghost preview with the new path length and the cost. Confirm to build.
  2. You can never seal the path completely. Walls and turrets make the road longer, which means more time under your guns.
  3. Hit Engage to start the wave. Speeds 1x to 4x are all free. You can keep building and upgrading during the wave.
  4. After each wave, open the wave report for per-turret damage and return, a damage heatmap and the coach tip. Unspent scrap earns interest, up to a cap.
  5. Every fifth wave you pick one of three doctrines. At Tier 3, a turret can specialise into one of two Tier-4 paths.

Modes

Campaign (five sectors, three difficulties), Academy (15 logic puzzles), Daily Protocol (same map for everyone, with a leaderboard) and Friend Challenge (create a code, your friend plays the same map and waves, highest score wins).

Playing a friend

In the game, tap Challenge a Friend, choose a sector and difficulty, and share the link or code. Your friend taps Challenge a Friend and pastes the code, or opens the link (it looks like https://hassma0k.github.io/aegis/c/?code=ABC123). No iPhone? They can play in the browser.

Purchases

Full Version, the Commanders, Double Blueprints and Founder's Pack are one-time purchases. Bought one on another device? Open the Store or Settings and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.

Languages

English, Arabic, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, German, French, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Italian and Turkish. Change it in Settings.
