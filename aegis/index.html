How it works

Three steps. A hundred ways to play them.

  1. Recon

    Read the wave

    Before every wave you see exactly what is coming, from Runners to armoured Breakers and flying Drones, and the glowing path from each portal to your core.

  2. Build the maze

    Bend the road

    Place barricades and turrets. A ghost preview shows the new path, the tiles gained and the cost before you confirm, so no fat-finger losses.

  3. Engage & learn

    Fight, then review

    Hit Engage, speed it up to 4x if you like, then open the wave report. Per-turret damage, kills and return, a heatmap, and a one-line coach tip.

Academy and coach

A tower defense that teaches you to think.

The Academy is 15 hand-made puzzles. Each has a fixed map and budget, and the obvious answer fails. You have to find the idea: buy time cheaply, use the rocks, match the tool to the problem. Then a short "Why it worked" card names it.

In the campaign, a coach reads your telemetry after every wave: how much damage armour ate, which turret earns the least, whether your path is long enough.

Example: if armour ate over a third of your kinetic damage, the coach points you to Strongest targeting or a Railgun.One short, rule-based tip per wave, so advice stays specific.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

How much does it cost?

The download is free and includes Sectors 1 and 2, Academy puzzles 1 to 6, the Daily Protocol, Friend Challenges and the Commanders Rook, Wrench and Frost. Full Version is $4.99 one time (Sectors 3 to 5, Academy 7 to 15, Endless, Sandbox). Volt, Lancer and Atlas are $2.99 each, or $5.99 for all three. Double Blueprints is $3.99. The Founder's Pack is $14.99 and bundles all of it, plus three colour themes and a Founder badge (worth $21.94 bought separately).

Do paid Commanders make me stronger?

No. Every Commander is balanced to the same power budget, so the paid ones play differently rather than better. Daily Protocol and Friend Challenges always use Rook for everyone, and Double Blueprints never touches leaderboards.

Are there ads, energy or subscriptions?

No. There are no ads, no energy or lives, no timers and no subscriptions. Every game speed (1x to 4x) is free.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 15 or later. The iPad layout uses a landscape sidebar instead of overlays. You can also try it in any modern browser at the web edition.

Which languages are supported?

8: English, Arabic (with a right-to-left interface), Japanese, Korean, Traditional Chinese, German, French and Brazilian Portuguese.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The campaign, Academy and Workshop play without a connection. Only the Daily leaderboard and Friend Challenges need the internet.

What about my privacy?

There is no account and no tracking, and no ads or analytics. The optional online features use an anonymous random device token and an auto-generated callsign. Read the privacy policy.

I bought an unlock on another device. How do I restore it?

Open the Store or Settings and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID.
