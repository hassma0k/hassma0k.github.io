Questions

Is H360 free?
The download is free and comes with 7 days of full access, with no card and no payment details. After that the app needs an H360 Plus subscription, monthly or yearly; the price is shown in the app before you subscribe. Nothing is charged until you choose a plan.
Do I need an Apple Watch?
No. Food logging, fasting, steps and challenges work with an iPhone alone. The Readiness score and sleep detail need an Apple Watch or another device that records sleep and overnight heart rate in Apple Health.
How accurate are calories from a photo?
They are estimates, not measurements. That is why H360 shows a calorie range instead of one number, asks you to confirm portions and oil or ghee, and lets you correct any item or add one the photo missed, by the gram.
How is fasting counted, and what about Ramadan?
Fasting hours are counted automatically from the gaps between the meals you log, with no timer. In Ramadan, Ramadan mode switches on by itself with the Umm al-Qura calendar, with fasting hours from Fajr to Maghrib for your city.
Does H360 give medical advice?
No. H360 is a wellness app. It is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat or prevent any disease. Its scores and food values are estimates. Talk to a qualified professional before changing your diet, exercise or fasting routine.
