The problem

Decisions change the statements. Few people see how.

A lease, an asset sale, an impairment or a new standard moves assets, liabilities, profit and ratios in ways that are easy to miss until the auditors, the lenders or the board ask. The standards run to hundreds of pages, and the explanations are written for specialists.

IFRS 18

IFRS 18 takes effect on 1 January 2027.

IFRS 18 is now in effect.

The new standard changes how the income statement is presented: new categories and subtotals, and a disclosure for management-defined performance measures such as adjusted EBITDA. Comparatives are restated, so preparation starts before the effective date.

Financial Controller explains the new structure in plain language and walks you through the classification questions.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. All content and every calculator runs on your device, so it works offline.

Does it use AI?

No. Explanations and simulations are written and reviewed by people and follow fixed rules, so you get the same answer every time.

What does it cost?

The app is free to download. Pro is a one-time purchase, no subscription. If you already bought Pro, use Restore Purchases in Settings on a new device.

Which standards and rules does it cover?

IFRS, including IFRS 18 and IFRS 19, together with SOCPA and ZATCA topics such as VAT, withholding tax and zakat.

Is this accounting or tax advice?

No. It is an educational reference. Always verify against the official standards and consult a qualified professional for your situation.

Which languages and appearances are supported?
