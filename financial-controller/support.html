Support

We usually reply within a few days.

Restore purchases

Bought Pro on another device or reinstalled the app? Open Settings, tap Restore Purchases and sign in with the same Apple ID you used to buy. Pro is a one-time purchase, so there is nothing to renew.

Switch language

Open Settings and choose English or Arabic. The layout flips to right-to-left automatically for Arabic. Light and dark appearance follow your phone, or you can set them in Settings.

Offline use

All content, simulators, wizards, calculators and checklists are stored in the app. No internet connection is needed. Only the App Store purchase and download need a connection.

Your data

The app has no accounts and sends nothing to us. Anything you enter is stored on your device only, so deleting the app deletes it. See the privacy policy.
