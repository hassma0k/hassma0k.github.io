Privacy Policy

Effective date: 11 November 2026

Summary

Financial Controller is an offline reference and calculator app. It collects no data about you.

What we do not collect

No account, name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos or advertising identifier. There are no ads, no analytics, no crash-reporting service and no tracking across other apps or websites.

What stays on your device

Your settings and anything you enter into the calculators, simulators and checklists are stored locally on your device only. We cannot see them, and deleting the app removes them.

Purchases

The optional Pro purchase is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details, name or Apple ID.

Children

The app contains no ads, no user-generated content and no data collection. It is an educational reference for finance professionals and students.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
