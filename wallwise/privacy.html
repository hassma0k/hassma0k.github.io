Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Wallwise is a tower defense game for iPhone, iPad and the web. We built it to collect as little as possible.

What we do not collect

No account, name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos or advertising identifier. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking across other apps or websites.

What stays on your device

Your progress, settings, Upgrades, Puzzle stars and any run in progress are saved on your device only.

Optional online features

The Daily Challenge leaderboard and Friend Challenges are optional and work only when you are online. When you use them, the game sends: an anonymous random device token created on your device (not linked to you or to your Apple ID), an auto-generated callsign chosen from a fixed word list (you cannot type free text), and the game data needed for the feature: the challenge code, zone, difficulty, seed and your scores. This data is stored with our server provider, Supabase. Nothing else is sent, and there is no chat.

Purchases

The optional one-time purchases (Full Version, Commanders, Double Gears and Founder's Pack) are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details, name or Apple ID.

Children

Wallwise is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. It has no ads and no user-generated text.

Your choices

You can play fully offline. To ask us to remove the online scores linked to your callsign, email us.
