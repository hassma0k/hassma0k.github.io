The 30-Year Tax Holiday

Effective tax rate · market access (Saudi RHQ programme)

3 min read · 3-question self-check · practise it in the app

Should you base a Regional HQ in Riyadh, and which functions belong onshore?

⚡In 30 seconds

  • A tax holiday only saves if you can afford the address.
  • Often the contract door, not the tax rate, carries the case.
  • Move functions one by one, on their own numbers.

🎯What you’ll learn

  • Explain what Saudi Arabia's RHQ programme offers and requires.
  • Weigh tax and contract access against higher running costs.
  • Judge function by function what belongs in Riyadh.

💡The idea

Think of a club membership. The discount, a 30-year 0% tax holiday, covers only qualifying RHQ activities. The card also opens a door: government contracts. The fee is real: higher running costs, relocation and local hiring.

A licensed RHQ needs real substance: senior people running the region from Riyadh. Since 2024, many government contracts are closed to multinationals without one. Often that access, not tax, carries the case. Other functions move only if their own numbers work.

🧭Decide like a CFO: Is the move worth it?

Your regional HQ pays 9% tax where it sits today. Riyadh offers 0% on qualifying activities and access to RHQ-only government bids, but costs more to run.

Stay put → keep lower costs and flexibility; the government-contract door stays closed.

Move for the tax alone → the saving doesn't cover the extra running and relocation costs.

Move the HQ, weight bids by win odds → positive, if the pipeline is real.

The call. Move the HQ. Government work, not tax, carries the case, so test the break-even win rate before committing.

🧮The formula, in plain words

Annual value of an RHQ

Net value = Tax saved + Contract margin − Extra running cost − Relocation capital × WACC − Localisation cost

Tax and realistic contract wins must beat the higher costs of operating from Riyadh.

⚠️Common mistakes

✗ Assuming the tax holiday covers all Saudi profits.

✓ It covers qualifying RHQ activities; model other profits at normal rates.

✗ Moving every function to maximise the incentive.

✓ Move a function only if its own numbers beat its extra cost.

✗ Counting the full contract pipeline as certain.

✓ Weight bids by realistic win odds and know your break-even rate.

✅Self-check

Three questions. Tap an answer to see why.

1Which description of Saudi Arabia's RHQ incentive is most accurate?

Income and withholding tax at 0% cover qualifying RHQ activities only, for a licensed RHQ with substance; sales offices don't qualify.

2Your RHQ case only turns positive if you win government contracts. How should you present the pipeline to the board?

The board needs the realistic value and how low the win rate can fall. Counting all bids overstates; ignoring them understates.

3Your HQ moves to Riyadh, securing RHQ status. Treasury sits in a 0%-tax hub and costs more in Riyadh. Move it?

Treasury's tax can't fall below 0% and the HQ already opens the contract door, so moving adds cost for nothing.

🔎Go deeper

⚖️ The trade-off

Onshore commits you to a higher cost base and a tight senior hiring market. A cheaper hub keeps flexibility but closes the government-contract door. Relocation is hard to reverse: you trade flexibility for access.

🧩 When the rule breaks

A speculative contract pipeline can turn the bridge negative. Non-qualifying income is taxed normally, and a nameplate office fails substance tests. Rules evolve. Firms with neither government customers nor much regional profit should not move.

🏢 In real companies

Saudi Arabia launched the RHQ programme under Vision 2030, licensed by the Ministry of Investment. Since 2024, government contracting has favoured, often required, a licensed RHQ. The real question is which functions belong in Riyadh. Conditions change: check current rules.

💼 In an interview

“Should a multinational move its regional headquarters to Riyadh?”

It is a financial decision. The Saudi RHQ programme offers a long 0% tax holiday on qualifying income and access to government contracts, against a higher operating cost and local hiring requirements. I would model the tax saving plus contract upside against the extra cost and the substance requirements, function by function. Move what creates value in Riyadh; do not move for the headline.

📖 Key terms
Regional Headquarters (RHQ)
A licensed Saudi entity managing a group's businesses across the region.
Qualifying activities
The HQ activities that get 0% income and withholding tax.
Substance
Real people and decisions located where the entity is registered.
